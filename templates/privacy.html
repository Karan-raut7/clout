1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Clout handles information associated with your account and use of the service.

By using Clout, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you create a Clout account, we currently collect information necessary to provide the service, including:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password in securely hashed form
  • Terms acceptance information

When you use chat functionality, Clout may also store messages associated with your account and the chat room in which they were sent.

3. Password Security

Clout does not need to store your password in plain text. Passwords are processed into a hashed form for account authentication.

You should still use a strong, unique password and avoid sharing your password with anyone.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by Clout may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Authenticate your account.
  • Provide chat and messaging functionality.
  • Associate messages with the appropriate account and room.
  • Maintain and improve the service.
  • Protect the security and integrity of Clout.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Chat Messages

Messages sent through Clout may be stored so that the service can provide chat history and related functionality.

Do not share passwords, financial information, authentication codes, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in chat rooms.

6. Information Sharing

Clout does not sell your personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and maintain the service.
  • Protect the security of Clout and its users.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unlawful activity.
  • Comply with a valid legal obligation or lawful request.

7. Data Storage

Account information and chat-related data may be stored on infrastructure used to operate Clout.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Clout may retain account and message information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce applicable agreements.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other forms of control over your data.

To make a privacy-related request, contact Clout using the contact information below.

10. Children's Privacy

Clout is not intended to be used in violation of applicable age restrictions or child-protection laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Clout in circumstances where collection was not permitted, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services

Clout may use third-party infrastructure or services necessary to operate the application. Such providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Clout.

However, transmission and storage of information over the internet cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Clout develops or as legal and operational requirements change.

Material changes may be communicated through Clout or by other reasonable means.
